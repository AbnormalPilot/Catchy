# Demo video plan (target 2:30, hard limit 3:00)

Judges score **Design, Potential Impact, Innovation, Presentation**, and they judge from the video, not by running the app. Every shot below is chosen to show one of those. The narration is yours: the cues say *what* to cover, not the words.

## Before recording

- [ ] Apple Intelligence on, and the model finished downloading (the card no longer says "used basic detection").
- [ ] Calendar, Reminders and Screen Recording already granted. Do one dry run, so no permission prompts appear on camera unless you want to show one.
- [ ] Catchy's icon visible in the menu bar; hide clutter. Turn auto-hide off for the recording so the icon is visible.
- [ ] iPhone on the same Apple ID with Handoff on (needed for Universal Clipboard). Have Calendar open on the iPhone, plus a screen-mirroring or camera shot of it.
- [ ] Props:
  - a real paper flyer, or one printed or on screen
  - a WhatsApp/Messages chat with a plan in it
  - the Devpost schedule line "Will It Ship, Thursday, Oct 22 at 3:00 pm ET"
  - a short schedule or syllabus
- [ ] Music: none, or royalty-free only (the rules forbid copyrighted music).
- [ ] Record at 1080p or higher (QuickTime › New Screen Recording, or ⇧⌘5). Upload to YouTube or Vimeo as public or unlisted.

## Where things can go wrong on camera

- **Universal Clipboard is slow or doesn't arrive:** keep Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on both devices and wait about 2 seconds after copying. Do one test copy just before recording.
- **The model gets a field wrong:** fix it on the card on camera. That shows the card is editable, which is the point.
- **The island closes:** it closes on any outside click. Don't click elsewhere while it's open.
- Grant Input Monitoring before recording so the ⌥⌘ tap works.
